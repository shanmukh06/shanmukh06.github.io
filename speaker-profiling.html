Parameter Efficient Speaker Profiling

Mixture of Recursions with Wav2Vec 2.0 for joint prediction of speaker age, height, and gender.

The problem

Speaker attributes can support useful real-world applications, but many approaches are parameter-heavy or fail to share information efficiently across tasks. This work explores a shared architecture that retains rich acoustic representations while keeping training and deployment practical.
