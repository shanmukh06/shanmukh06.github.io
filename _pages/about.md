I am a third-year undergraduate student at [UPES](https://www.upes.ac.in) with a strong interest in Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Speech AI research.

Previously, I worked as a Research Intern at the [Department of ECE, NITK Surathkal](ece.nitk.ac.in), I was advised by [Prof. Deepu Vijayasenan](http://ece.nitk.ac.in/faculty/deepu-vijayasenan). My research focused on speaker profiling, developing efficient deep learning architectures for estimating speaker attributes such as age, height, and gender from speech.

My current research interests span Speech Foundation Models, Speaker Profiling, ML Systems, and Efficient Deep Learning. I am particularly interested in improving the efficiency and scalability of large-scale models through GPU programming, with a focus on [CUDA](https://developer.nvidia.com/cuda), [JAX](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/index.html), and low-level kernel optimization. Looking ahead, I hope to explore broader directions at the intersection of foundation models, world models, and high-performance AI systems.

Beyond research, I enjoy learning about venture capital and technology investing, particularly in AI, semiconductors, and emerging technologies.

Outside of academics, I enjoy playing narrative-driven AAA games such as the Batman: Arkham series, Resident Evil, and GTA. My current favorite game is 007 First Light. I also enjoy listening to Tamil and Telugu music on Spotify.
